# Label IQ

### Intelligent Legal Metrology Verification System

> **Know what you're buying. Understand what's inside. Verify what's on the label.**

Label IQ is an intelligent product-label analysis system developed for **Smart India Hackathon 2026** under Problem Statement **SIH26034**.

The project aims to make packaged-product information more accessible and understandable to consumers by analysing product labels and bringing together the information printed across different sides of a package.

Instead of manually searching through every panel of a product, Label IQ provides a unified view of the important information present on the label.

---

## The Problem

When purchasing a packaged product, important information is often distributed across multiple sides of the package.

Consumers may need to look for:

- Mandatory product declarations
- Manufacturer, packer or importer details
- Net quantity
- Maximum Retail Price (MRP)
- Country of origin
- Manufacturing or packing information
- Ingredients
- Nutritional information
- Allergen-related information
- Other relevant product details

This information can be difficult to locate, interpret and verify quickly.

Label IQ addresses this gap by turning package labels into structured and understandable insights.

---

## Our Solution

Label IQ allows users to scan a packaged product and analyse the information available across its different surfaces.

The system focuses on two major areas:

### Legal Metrology Verification

Label IQ helps identify and verify important declarations required on packaged commodities.

It brings together information from the package and presents the relevant compliance findings in a clear format.

### Ingredient & Health Clarity

Label IQ analyses ingredient information to help consumers better understand what is present in a product.

It can provide meaningful health-related insights and highlight relevant concerns such as:

- High sugar
- High sodium
- Common allergens
- Other identified ingredient-related factors

The objective is to move beyond simply reading a label and help users **understand it**.

---

## Key Features

### Multi-Side Product Analysis

Analyse information from different sides of a product package and bring it together into a unified product view.

### Legal Metrology Verification

Check relevant package declarations and identify missing, inconsistent or potentially problematic information.

### Ingredient Analysis

Convert complex ingredient information into clearer and more understandable insights.

### Warning Labels

Highlight relevant ingredient and health-related concerns through easy-to-understand warnings.

### 🔍 Product Information

Bring important package information together so users don't have to manually search through every side of the product.

### 📝 Consumer Petition Box

Give consumers a simple way to report label discrepancies or missing statutory information.
